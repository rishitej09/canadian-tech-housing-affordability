# Canadian Tech Jobs & Housing Affordability Analysis

**Course:** DAMO-699-1 (Fall 2026 Capstone Project — Master of Data Analytics)
**Instructor:** Prof. Bilal Eltoufaili 
---

## 📌 What We Are Trying to Achieve
Relocating to a new city requires balancing career opportunities against the local cost of living. This capstone project evaluates and compares **employment opportunities, advertised salaries, and rental housing costs** across Canada's top three technology markets:
* **Toronto, ON**
* **Vancouver, BC**
* **Montréal, QC**

---

## 🎯 Core Research Questions
**Main Question:**  
> *To what extent do advertised salaries for data-related occupations align with rental housing costs across Toronto, Vancouver, and Montréal?*

1. How do advertised salaries and job availability for **Data Analyst**, **Data Scientist**, and **Data Engineer** roles vary across the three cities?
2. How do monthly rental costs vary across **Toronto**, **Vancouver**, and **Montréal**?
3. Which combinations of **job position and city** offer the most favorable relationship between salary and rental cost?

---

## 🗂️ Planned Data Sources
* **Job Postings:** Adzuna API & Jooble API *(Also: Indeed & LinkedIn)*
* **Rental Listings:** Rentsync LIFT System API *(Also: Rentals.ca & Realtor.ca)*
* **Official Benchmarks:** Statistics Canada, Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC), and Government of Canada Job Bank
